<p>Native Android client for your self-hosted <b>Uptime Kuma 2.x</b> server. See the status of all monitors at a glance, dig into response times and certificates, manage monitors, maintenance windows and status pages, and get alerted on your phone through your own <b>ntfy</b> server via <b>UnifiedPush</b>.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Live dashboard with heartbeat bars, uptime, groups, tags, search and filters</li>
<li>Monitor details: response-time chart, uptime 24 h / 30 d / 1 y, certificate expiry, important events</li>
<li>Create and edit monitors of every type, manage tags, pause, resume and delete</li>
<li>Push alerts via ntfy and UnifiedPush, per-monitor mute, quiet hours, alert history</li>
<li>Maintenance windows and status pages including incidents</li>
<li>Home screen widget and Quick Settings tile</li>
<li>Login with two-factor authentication, works offline with the last known state</li>
<li>Material 3 with dynamic color and dark mode, English and German</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Requirements</b>: an Uptime Kuma 2.x server. For push alerts a self-hosted ntfy server and the ntfy app as UnifiedPush distributor.</p>

<p><b>Privacy</b>: no tracking, no analytics, no ads, no Google services. The app only talks to your Kuma server and, for push, to your ntfy server.</p>

<p>This is an unofficial client and not affiliated with the Uptime Kuma project.</p>
